# SD-Rules-v1_2026_10_14

Responsible-use rules for the Beneficiary Service Desk. **These are the five rules written in Lab 4.**
**Owner:** Your Name · **Version:** v1 · **Date:** 2026_10_14

Each rule says who, what and when, and can be answered with yes or no.

| # | Area | Rule | Yes / no test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data | Before pasting an inquiry, the agent replaces every name, number and phone with a code such as User-1, Request-X or Phone-1. | Did I replace all of them before pasting? |
| 2 | Verification | Before sending, the agent scores the reply with the five-point verification checklist and sends only a reply that scores 5 out of 5. | Did the reply score 5 out of 5? |
| 3 | Fairness | Before a template enters the library, its owner runs the swap test and records that no unexplained difference remains. | Is the swap-test result recorded? |
| 4 | Policy | The agent uses only AI tools the organization approves, and the template version and the approver are recorded for every reply sent. | Is the tool approved, and are version and approver recorded? |
| 5 | Human decision | AI never decides eligibility, approval or the outcome of a complaint: a person decides these cases before any reply is sent. | Did a person decide before the reply went out? |
